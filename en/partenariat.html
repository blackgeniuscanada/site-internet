Our mission

Identify, equip, document.

Every child starts with an honest academic diagnostic. From there: an individualized plan, targeted competitions, rigorous follow-up — and a documented file that proves, by the end of the journey, the road travelled.

Our five values: excellence, rigour, solidarity, identity, transparency. See the full mission →

Our motto

"We're not preparing students. We're building a generation."

By 2035: a documented cohort of excellence, admitted to the world's best institutions, proudly carrying the names of their families and communities of origin.

What we do

A complete program, led by families.

Tutoring, competitions, coding, languages, summer workshops, mentorship — nine pedagogical strands built around a flagship program, the BlackGenius Math Game.

01

Academic tutoring

Weekly support in math, science, French and English, in small groups by grade level.

02

Competition prep

Targeted training for math, science and coding competitions, and preparation for enriched programs (IEP, IB).

03

BlackGenius Math Game

Our flagship program: an online app that turns math practice into a game, available to every child in our nonprofit.

04

STEM summer workshops

Two intensive sessions in July and August: science projects, experiments, design thinking workshops.

05

University mentorship

"Mentor Time": our youth meet Black Canadian professionals in medicine, engineering, law, finance and research.

06

School services · 2028

Upcoming offer for partner schools: clubs, workshops, dropout-prevention days.

Ways to get involved

Several partnership formats.

Every organization contributes according to its means and strengths. Here are the main ways to collaborate with us.

Financial

Sponsor an event

Targeted support: scholarships, summer workshops, Math Game, or one of our signature events.

In kind

Equipment, space, services

Venues for an event, IT equipment, professional services (legal, accounting, design).

Expertise

Mentorship and volunteering

Black Canadian professionals invited to become mentors, executive coaches, competition judges, or speakers at our events.

Institutional

Partner school or organization

Collaboration with schools, universities and community organizations — starting 2028 for the dedicated school offer.
