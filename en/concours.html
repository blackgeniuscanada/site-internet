Math and logic contests.

Contests you can register your child for yourself, no school required — in Quebec, across Canada, and online internationally. Registration dates, contest dates and costs, kept up to date.

Mathematics

Canada · Grades 1–12

Math Kangaroo (Canada)

The world's largest school competition. Individual registration online or at a test centre — you register your child yourself, no school involvement needed.

Registration :from September 2026 (reduced fee before December)Contest :March 18, 2027
Register directly · from $20 Register
International · Online, grades 2–8

Noetic Learning Math Contest — At-Home Edition

A recognized US competition for elementary and middle school. The "at-home" edition is designed for students without a school team.

Registration :before October 22, 2026Contest :November 27 – December 4, 2026
Register directly · $29 Register
Quebec · From grade 3

Championnat international des jeux mathématiques et logiques

Organized by AQJM and Université Laval: the qualifying round is free, individual and open to everyone — no school required.

Registration :from November 1, 2026Contest :qualifying round through January 15, 2027
Register directly · Free Register
International · Online, high school

Purple Comet! Math Meet

Free online competition, in teams of 1 to 6 students. A parent can act as supervisor and register their child under the "homeschooled" category.

Registration :from February 1, 2027Contest :April 6–15, 2027
Register directly · Free Register
